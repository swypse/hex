# Hex

A 2D turn-based strategy game on a hex map. Build and upgrade villages, train
warriors, riders, archers, swordsmen, knights, and catapults, research skills
(climbing, water, forestry, science, shields, riding, and more), and explore a
procedurally generated world. Conquer rival tribes by capturing their villages
or score the most points by the final turn — solo against AI or with friends in
online multiplayer.
